# /position

Goal: existing evidence → Capability Profile → Role Hypothesis Pool. No JD required.

1. Load [shared contracts](../schemas/contracts.md), [discovery rules](../rules/discovery.md) and [Capability Profile](../schemas/capability-profile.md). Read existing Career profile, relevant evidence and preferences first; record unavailable exact originals/versions. Respect completed-only cutoff and source provenance. Never replace owners.
2. Build the bounded output inventory from actual completed work and per-phase Human/AI/team contributions. Look at what was produced and tested before degree or title. Exclude unsupported engineering, design craft, medical authority and commercial results.
3. Compare against existing capability entries before adding. Identify repeated behavior across materially distinct contexts; distinguish direct verification from report-derived inference. Use the four epistemic statuses, explicit limitations and categorical confidence. A single weak tool/course action stays POTENTIAL. Preserve explicit self-claims as such. Do not auto-adopt discoveries.
4. Counter-check every supplied seed role. Explain supports, counters, gaps, plausible misunderstanding and adjacent alternatives. Unknown candidate motivation stays UNKNOWN. Generate a small useful pool with [role hypotheses](../schemas/role-hypothesis.md), from explicit capabilities, hidden patterns and repeated problems as appropriate. No seed or exact title is required: describe work responsibilities first and propose title vocabulary as inference.
5. Keep industry open and preferences distinct from qualification. Apply MT gate. No role recommendation on offline evidence alone. Route the pool to [/discover](discover.md) for search strategy and validation; actual supplied job gates belong to [/analyse-job](analyse-job.md).
6. Produce one readable review candidate in contract order, surfacing decisive counters/unknowns rather than reciting policy. Ask only material questions under the stop rule; existing evidence may be sufficient with zero questions. End with a bounded validation next step, not a career interview or application package.
